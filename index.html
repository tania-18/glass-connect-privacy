Privacy Policy for Glass Connect

Last updated: October 2026

Glass Connect accesses your Google Calendar and Google Tasks solely to display your schedule and sync to-do lists on your smart mirror interface.

Data Collection & Storage

  • We do not sell, store, or share your private Google data with third parties.
  • Calendar events and tasks are processed ephemerally to display your daily schedule.
  • Authentication data is securely managed via Supabase.
